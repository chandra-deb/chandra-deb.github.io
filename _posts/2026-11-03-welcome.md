This is a sample post. It exists so the homepage, tag pages, and post layout have something to display while the blog is being set up.

## Writing posts

Every post is a Markdown file in `_posts`. The filename supplies the publish date and URL:

```text
_posts/2026-11-03-welcome.md  →  /welcome/
```

Front matter at the top of the file holds the title, description, and tags.

## What you get

- Tag filtering on the [tags page](/tags/)
- An [RSS feed](/feed.xml)
- Automatic sitemap and SEO metadata
- Light and dark styles that follow your system preference

> Replace this post with your own writing. The house rules live in `WRITING.md` in the repository.

Happy writing.
